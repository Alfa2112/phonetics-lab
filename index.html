How it works

  1. Sign in

    Pick your name from the class list, or type it in.

  2. Take the tour

    A short guide opens the first time you sign in. Skip it whenever you like.

  3. Learn and record

    Tap a sound, listen, then record yourself. Work through vowels, diphthongs and consonants.

  4. Play and save

    Pass the five quiz levels, then download your portfolio.
